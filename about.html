Who we are

Building a world where no one is left behind.

Saint Moses Relief Organization is a Canadian charity established in 2012 — though our work in Egypt began years earlier under a different name, improving living conditions and bringing hope to communities. In 2016 we expanded into North & South Sudan.

Across more than two decades of activity, SMRO has directed hundreds of millions in aid, supported over 30,000 people, and engaged 500+ volunteers across 22 districts in Egypt and 2 regions in Sudan. Every dollar is directed to the people who need it most.

Our Mission

Help as many people as we can escape poverty in Egypt, Sudan & South Sudan.

Our Vision

Thriving communities of independent, well-fed families — with education and clean water for all.
